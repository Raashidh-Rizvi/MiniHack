Selected Sri Lankan Problem & Proposed Solution

3.1 The Problem

Across Sri Lanka's 14,022 Grama Niladhari (GN) divisions, neighborhood public infrastructure hazards — potholes, blocked drains, burst water mains, broken streetlights, garbage accumulation — routinely sit unresolved for weeks or months. The root causes are:

  • Paper Bureaucracy: Residents must physically visit Pradeshiya Sabha or GN offices to submit handwritten petitions which are easily misplaced with zero digital tracking.
  • Fragmented Channels: Citizens often do not know which authority (RDA, CEB, NWSDB, or local council) is responsible for a given issue category, leading to misdirected or abandoned complaints.
  • No Objective Prioritization: Municipal maintenance units have no data-driven method to rank which problems are most critical, resulting in arbitrary repair schedules where high-risk hazards are deprioritized.
  • Monsoon Amplification: Sri Lanka's tropical rainfall turns unresolved drainage issues into flash flooding, dengue mosquito breeding grounds, and road washouts — compounding urgency every wet season.

3.2 The Proposed Solution — GramaFix

GramaFix is a responsive civic-tech web application that bridges citizens and municipal authorities through transparent, data-driven technology. It provides five core capabilities:

  • Fast Mobile-First Reporting: Any citizen can report a community hazard in under 60 seconds from a smartphone using a validated intake form.
  • Deterministic Community Priority Score: Eliminates political bias by computing an objective 0–100 ranking for every report.
  • Community Upvoting Feed: Residents endorse existing reports to prevent duplicate filings and amplify collective urgency.
  • Officer Resolution Pipeline: Department Officers manage their assigned-work queue with transparent status progression.
  • System Administrator Triage: Global dashboard for officer assignment, severity adjustment, moderation, and KPI monitoring.
⚙ Community Priority Score Algorithm
Priority Score = (Severity × 0.40) + (Affected Population × 0.30) + (Urgency × 0.20) + (Report Age × 0.10)

Each component normalized to 0–100 before weighting.

Score Bands:  0–25 = LOW  |  26–50 = MEDIUM  |  51–75 = HIGH  |  76–100 = CRITICAL
